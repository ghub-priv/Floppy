How Combined Intelligence works

Movies and TV are analysed separately, then compared. That keeps the comparison fair: thousands of film ratings cannot overpower your TV history, and thousands of episode ratings cannot overpower your films.

Combined score: movies and TV get an equal 50% say. A preference only appears here when there is enough evidence for it in both.
